Mushafna Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Mushafna (مصحفنا) is a mobile app that helps you read the Quran every day, on your own or with a group. It is made by Munni Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what is needed to save your reading progress and run your groups. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking. Your voice never leaves your phone.

You can use the app without an account. In that case your reading progress stays on your phone only and nothing is sent to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Some things are stored only on your phone and never sent to us: your app settings (such as language, theme, preferred reciter and daily reminder time), and all of your reading if you use the app without signing in. Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone. If you later sign in on that phone, your reading so far is added to your new account.

Microphone and audio

Recite mode. When you choose to recite, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone, using a model built into the app. Your voice is processed as you speak and then discarded. It is never recorded to a file, never uploaded, and never sent to us or anyone else. Only the result is kept: which verses you completed, which counts toward your reading like any other mode. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Listen mode. Recitations are streamed from a public Quran audio service, Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network). To deliver the audio, that service receives your phone's IP address and which verse is being played, as any website would. We do not send it your account or any other information about you.

How we use and share information

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, save and sync your reading, count your daily pages, and show group progress. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Members of your groups can see your display name, how many pages you read each day, and your time zone (used to work out when your day starts). They cannot see your email address, where you are in the Quran, or which verses you read. Anyone with a group's invite code can join that group, so share codes only with people you trust. The group's manager can remove members.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Settings, Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile, reading position, reading history and daily totals, and removes you from all your groups. A group is deleted when its last member leaves. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's routine backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

Leaving a group removes you from it straight away; your own reading stays in your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each person can read only their own data and the limited group information described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, but accounts are not intended for children under 13 without a parent's involvement. Children can use the app without signing in, in which case nothing is sent to us. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see and change your display name in the app, and delete your account and all its data at any time. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK, or California), you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or object to how we use it. To make a request, email us at munnistudio.apps@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where EU law applies, we process your data because it is needed to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the change is significant, for example collecting a new kind of information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
